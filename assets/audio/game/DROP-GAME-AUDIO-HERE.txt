Optional Woodland Search audio hooks

Use JackLight Narrator TTS or short musical jingles and save the MP3 files in this folder.
The game will use them automatically when the filenames below are present; missing files are safely ignored.

voice-welcome.mp3       Spoken welcome after “Enter the woods”
voice-well-done.mp3     Spoken celebration after a character is found
voice-find-next.mp3     Spoken prompt when the next character is revealed
jingle-hint.mp3         Short sound when the hint button is pressed
jingle-transition.mp3   Short gentle sound between woodland scenes

Keep these recordings short (around one to three seconds for jingles) and family-friendly.
